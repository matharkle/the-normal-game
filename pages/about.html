ABOUT

The Normal Game was inspired by my experiences playing video games as a person with color blindness. Often times there are items, visual effects, or other critical game componets that requre players to make differentiations solely based on color. When I inevitably am unable to do so easily, this leads to confusion, frustration, and makes me feel incapable.

I wanted to recreate that experience for others by making levels that are confusing, nonsensical, or just impossible. My hope is to bring awareness to what me and many other people go through. (About 1 of every 10-12 men are colorblind and 1 of every 200 women). There is a widespread lack of accessible design in the gaming industry largely due to lack of awareness or poor knowledge of ways to accomodate.
